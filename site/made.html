Adding your board

We're working on opening this page to everyone's boards. The plan is that you'll add one with a pull request that names your repository, Via's checks run on it, and it shows up here once it passes. It's item 18 on our roadmap. Until then, we'd like to see what you're making in our Discord.

Every board here so far is one of ours. Herald and Rosetta aren't here yet, because we haven't seen them pass every check. They'll show up when they do.

A board here passed Via's checks on its design files. That doesn't mean anyone has built it or tested it.
